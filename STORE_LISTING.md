## Product name

MP4 to MP3 & Audio Converter

## Short description

Batch convert HEIC, WebP and AVIF photos to JPG or PNG. Offline, nothing uploaded.

## Description

iPhone photos arrive as HEIC. Images saved from the web arrive as WebP or AVIF. Then a website, a printer or an older app refuses them.

MP4 to MP3 & Audio Converter turns them into ordinary JPG or PNG files. Drop photos or whole folders on the window, choose the format, and press Convert. It works offline: your photos never leave your PC.

It converts for free, three photos per run, with no trial that runs out and no watermark. An optional one-off unlock (£4.99, bought on our website) removes the three-photo limit for large batches.

What it reads: HEIC, HEIF, AVIF, JXL, WebP, JPG, PNG, BMP, TIFF and GIF.
What it writes: JPG, PNG or lossless WebP.

Useful details:
- Convert a whole folder, including its subfolders, in one go.
- Choose JPG quality, and optionally shrink photos to a maximum size for email or upload.
- Save next to the originals or into a folder you pick.
- Never overwrites anything. If a name is taken, the new file gets "(2)".
- Keeps each photo the right way up and keeps its date.
- Uses all your processor cores, so large batches finish quickly.

About HEIC: Windows reads HEIC through Microsoft's own HEIF and HEVC extensions, which many PCs already have. If yours doesn't, the app tells you exactly which extension is missing and opens its Store page. The HEIF extension is free; Microsoft charges a small one-off price for the HEVC one.

No account, no ads, no telemetry.

## Privacy

Stored locally: settings (output format, quality, output folder, licence key)
in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mp3Converter\settings.json. Nothing else is kept.

Network: a version check against GitHub Releases about once a day, which the
user can turn off. No images, file names or usage data are sent anywhere.
